# Synthetic service inputs

All fixtures are generated by application/service_examples.py and explicitly labelled
synthetic. USD/EUR conditional model rates and FX, one FX-forward legal book and daily
USD CSA are illustrative inputs, not observed market data or saved risk outputs.
portfolio.json, market.json, pricing-context.json and calibration.json use a descriptive
fixture envelope; unwrap data when using /v1 ingestion/command payloads. Full live workflow:
[service example](../../../docs/api/EXAMPLES.md). No source commit or accuracy claim is inferred.
